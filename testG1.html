Do I Have a Right?

GAME ORIGINALLY BY ICIVICS CHECK OUT THE WEBSITE>>
Play Fullscreen

In Do I Have a Right? you can run your own firm of lawyers who specialize in constitutional law. Decide if potential clients have a right, match them with the best lawyer, and win their case. The more clients you serve and the more cases you win, the faster your law firm grows!

Play Do I Have a Right? in:

  • The full edition or a Bill of Rights edition
  • English language or Spanish language

Hear or mute English voiceover, music, and/or sound effects in the setting menu in the game.

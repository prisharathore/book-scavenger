Hello fellow scavengers...

This is a guide to how to play the book scavenger game! So on ciphers page you can team up with other scavengers and solve ciphers to uncover ebooks there will be a book of the day and a word of the day, you will level up once you have collected a certain number of points from uncovering books! You can also chat with friends and collaberate with them with parent permission. Check in for the seasonal challenges in which you can compete with other players in teams to uncover a certain book first Make sure to climb up the leaderboard as much as you can. Good Luck!
